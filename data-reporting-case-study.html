Challenge and response

Turning separate submissions into one reliable management view.

01

The reporting problem

Recurring submissions can arrive in different file structures, date formats, naming conventions, and completeness levels. Without a controlled consolidation process, management reporting becomes slower, harder to verify, and vulnerable to duplicate or inconsistent entries.

02

My role

I consolidate submitted records, standardize fields, validate completeness and consistency, identify issues requiring follow-up, prepare summary tables and charts, and translate the results into concise narrative findings and presentation materials.

03

The reporting solution

A structured master table combines unit submissions, applies consistent labels and dates, flags missing or questionable records, produces status summaries, and supports corrective-action monitoring through traceable source information.

Fifteen of 18 units submitted all three selected report types within the prescribed period.
