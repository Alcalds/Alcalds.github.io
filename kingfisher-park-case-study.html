Project overview

Designed around real client needs.

Kingfisher Park needed an organized visitor-facing website that explains beach activities, BERAE nature tours and what guests should know before arriving. I implemented page structure and content in Wix Studio while configuring the existing native booking experience and bespoke interactive scenes.

My role

Website design and implementation in Wix Studio; responsive layout work; front-end visual interactions and Velo integrations; service/category/form and calendar configuration; site-content updates, SEO setup and ongoing troubleshooting.

Work delivered / developed

  • Visitor-focused multi-page architecture with seven editorial pages and a separate native Book Online journey
  • Custom HOME experience with daylight, golden-hour and after-dark scene transitions, manual controls and animated visual effects
  • Native Wix Bookings service listings, service details, schedules, booking forms and participant-related policy setup
  • Updated mobile/tablet layouts, service descriptions, visit guidance and website content
  • SEO titles, page descriptions and search-visibility checklist work

Implementation

How the work is structured.

The site integrates design, copy and booking operations rather than treating them as unrelated features. Visual interactions use custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript patterns with Wix Studio and Velo, while operational booking is handled by Wix Bookings.

01

Visitor journey

Planned clear paths between park information, experiences, the visitor guide and the Book Online service list.

02

Interactive home

Implemented three-scene hero behavior and refined controls, transitions and responsiveness.

03

Booking configuration

Set up four beach activity products and two BERAE tour products with distinct visitor booking forms and scheduling workflows.

04

Ongoing quality control

Reviewed mobile rendering, staffing conflicts, tidal-window scheduling, booking policy behavior and SEO metadata.

Scope and status

Clear claims, clear boundaries.

Tools and approach

Wix Studio, responsive design, Wix Velo, HTML, CSS and JavaScript for supported custom interactions, content and visual quality assurance.

What is not claimed as completed

  • Full online payment integration and paid checkout have not been validated as completed.
  • Special BERAE group-tier pricing and child rates still need end-to-end checkout validation.
  • Final phone-landscape layout refinement remains an ongoing QA task.
  • Kayak safety and operational availability are determined by park staff, not automated by this site.
