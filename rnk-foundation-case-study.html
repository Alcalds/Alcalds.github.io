Project overview

Designed around real client needs.

The Regalo ng Kilit Foundation website is being developed to communicate its work through an engaging multi-page structure. The homepage centers on a four-chapter visual narrative with animated transitions and interactive chapter navigation. Additional editorial sections are being developed in native Wix Studio.

My role

Website layout and content architecture, custom hero interaction, layering and visibility behavior, Velo and custom CSS/JavaScript implementation, iterative responsive testing.

Work delivered / developed

  • Four interactive hero chapters with chapter navigation controls
  • Custom animation timing and scene transitions
  • Layered hero content with navigation and clickability refinements
  • Ongoing page-by-page development in Wix Studio
  • Responsive layout review and component-level troubleshooting
